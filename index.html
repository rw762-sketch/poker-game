    WELCOME TO THE ROOM

    Make your best five.

    Combine your two private cards with the five community cards to make the strongest five-card hand.

    Check to pass when no bet is owed. Call to match a bet, raise to increase it, or fold to leave the hand.

    Community cards arrive in three stages: flop (3), turn (1), river (1). The last player standing or best hand at showdown wins the pot.

    Strongest to weakest
    Straight flush → Four of a kind → Full house → Flush → Straight → Three of a kind → Two pair → Pair → High card

    Each player begins with 1,000 chips. Blinds are 10 / 20. The dealer rotates every hand. Opponents use simple automated strategies.
