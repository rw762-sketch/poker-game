    WELCOME TO THE ROOM

    Make your best five.

    Combine your two private cards with the five community cards to make the strongest five-card hand.

    Check to pass when no bet is owed. Call to match a bet, raise to increase it, or fold to leave the hand.

    Community cards arrive in three stages: flop (3), turn (1), river (1). The last player standing or best hand at showdown wins the pot.

    Strongest to weakest
    Straight flush → Four of a kind → Full house → Flush → Straight → Three of a kind → Two pair → Pair → High card

    Each player begins with 1,000 chips. Blinds are 10 / 20. The dealer rotates every hand. In solo play, choose Easy, Medium, or Hard above the table. Changes take effect next hand. Medium and Hard use position, board texture, and estimated opponent hand ranges based on public bets. Hard uses parameters tuned on simulated sample games and adapts to public betting history across hands. Neither reads opponents’ hidden cards or the deck. These are casual practice opponents, not professional poker solvers. In online rooms, the host deals each hand. Each turn has a 45-second limit: a timeout checks when possible, otherwise folds. New players join before the first hand; returning players can reconnect to their seat. Refreshing or closing the host tab ends the room.
