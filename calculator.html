    What these numbers can—and can’t—tell you

    The simulator deals unseen cards without replacement. It samples compatible opponent starting hands from your chosen range and deals the remaining board. Known opponent cards replace that range.

    Ranges are coarse model assumptions and do not automatically capture every action an opponent has taken. The confidence interval measures sampling noise only, not errors in your range assumptions.

    Position affects your available information and the suggested strategy. It does not magically improve the cards. Bet suggestions also consider board texture, minimum raises, and your remaining stack.
